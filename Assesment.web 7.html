The Bogdon problem

Bogdon problem one of the 3 Doomsday heists Lester and Avon asks you to launch an operation on a foreign submarine lurking off the coast. One team boarrds through the airlock and disables the sub, while the other holds off enemy agents in an aircraft above.

The full payout for this heist on hard mode with first time weekly bonus is $1,959,019 and the payout with cuts is if 55% for host is $1,077,460 and for the crew's cut is 45% if with duo is $881,558 and if you do it with a squad you do 40-20-20-20 host gets $783,607 thousand and for the crew they get $391,803 each.
