Fleeca Job Heist

Summary

The Fleeca Job is one of the 5 classic apartment heists. It acts as a tutorial heist where Lester trains you for the bigger jobs that follow.

Payout

For a first-time weekly completion on easy mode the payout is about $151,000, on normal mode about $302,000, and on hard mode it is around $377,000. If you do the split with 85%-15% on hard mode, the crew gets $56,000 and the host gets $319,000.
