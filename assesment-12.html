Kortz Center Heist

Summary

The Kortz Center Heist begins when Mr. Fabor calls the protagonist about an art gallery where he wants you to steal the priceless painting inside the vault and beyond.

Payout

The payout for the primary target painting is around $1,600,000 on hard mode with the first-time weekly bonus.

The payout for the secondary target loot in the main exhibition gallery is around $100,000 even with the buyer's request.

The payout for the secondary target loot in the second-floor exhibit is around $100,000 to $200,000 with the buyer's request.

The payout for the secondary target loot in the vault is around $75,000 without the buyer's request, but around $100,000 with it.
