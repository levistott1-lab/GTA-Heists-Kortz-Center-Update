Prison Break

Summary

Prison Break is one of the 5 classic apartment heists in GTA 5 Online. Lester wants you to do it because he has an outside job offer to break Maxim Rashkovsky out of jail.

Payout

The weekly bonus full payout on easy mode is $525,000, on normal mode it is $1,050,000, and on hard mode it is $1,312,500. With a 40-20-20-20 split, the host gets $210,000 on easy mode, $420,000 on normal mode, and $525,000 on hard mode, while each crew member gets $105,000 on easy mode and $210,000 on normal mode, or $262,000 on hard mode.
