Humane Lab Raid

Summary

Humane Labs Raid is one of the 5 classic apartment heists. Lester acts as a broker for the job because he receives a lucrative finder's fee from Agent 14.

Payout

If you take a 40% cut on hard mode and get the weekly bonus, the host receives $590,625 while the crew gets $295,000.
