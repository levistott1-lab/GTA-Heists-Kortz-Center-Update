Series A Funding

Summary

Series A Funding is one of the 5 classic apartment heists. Trevor Phillips wants you to help him because he is trying to do a drug deal but does not have the money, so he asks you to steal it from rival companies.

Payout

The full payout for this heist on hard mode with the first-time weekly bonus is $1,330,000. With a 40% cut, the host receives $353,500 and each crew member receives $176,750.
