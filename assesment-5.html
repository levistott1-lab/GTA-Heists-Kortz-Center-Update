The Pacific Standard Job

Summary

Pacific Standard is one of the 5 classic apartment heists. Lester asks you to hit the flagship branch of one of the biggest banks in the city to secure a massive payout.

Payout

The full payout for this heist on hard mode with the first-time weekly bonus is $5,625,000. With a 45% cut, the host receives $2,531,250 and each crew member receives $843,750.
