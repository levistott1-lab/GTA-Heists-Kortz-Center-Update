The Doomsday Scenario

Summary

Doomsday Scenario is one of the 3 Doomsday heists. Lester and Bogdan tell you to go and stop Avon and Clifford from launching a rocket that could destroy the entire planet.

Payout

The full payout for this heist on hard mode with the first-time weekly bonus is $2,250,000. With a 55% share, the host receives $1,167,500. If you do it as a duo, the crew's cut is 45% and each member gets $1,062,500. If you do it with a squad using the 40-20-20-20 split, the host gets $950,000 and each crew member gets $450,000.
