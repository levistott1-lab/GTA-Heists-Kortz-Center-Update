Diamond Casino Heist

Summary

The Diamond Casino Heist begins with the Cheng family telling you to infiltrate the most secure building in Los Santos.

Cash Payout

The full payout for this heist with cash on hard mode and the first-time bonus is $2,302,000. With a 55% share, the host gets $1,266,100 and each crew member gets $1,035,900 in a duo. With a 40-20-20-20 split, the host gets $920,800 and each crew member gets $460,400.
